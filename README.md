One dad joke, at most 200 characters, for the [Dad Joke Showdown](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mfnakmal-dad-joke-showdown-1d2ce3e5) bounty on make.fun.

## The joke

> I asked my dad why he keeps a ladder in the kitchen. He said, 'For high tea.'

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the joke to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(the grader's `/output` mount). Nothing else runs. The joke is the whole entry.
